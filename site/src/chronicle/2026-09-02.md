# 2026-09-02

**The rest of book one was drafted in a single long session, which finished the book.**

David asked for everything after the published scenes to be drafted in one sustained push, with instructions to stop if quality visibly slipped rather than push through regardless. Over roughly fifty commits in under three hours, the second act was redrafted from scratch and the third act, plotted out fresh with its own scene structure, was drafted alongside it. Each scene went through the same pipeline settled on over the previous few days: a draft, a canon-consistency check, a craft-quality review, and a check against the overlap tool before committing.

A few prose-habit corrections came out of the day's work. The rate of a particular reasoning connective in the narration was found running too high against its target and brought down, with the underlying reasoning kept but recast into shorter sentences or different connecting words rather than simply cut. A repeated phrase describing where things "went off" across six separate instances was varied while keeping the actual place names intact. Several pairs of scenes that had drifted into repeating each other's phrasing were caught by the overlap tool and reworked.

With the last scene drafted, David had Claude update the project's status tracking and write a short handoff note for whoever picked this up next, closing out the drafting phase nine days after the first line of narrator's voice was written.
