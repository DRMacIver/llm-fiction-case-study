# 2026-08-30

**A full second act, dozens of scenes, was drafted and then thrown away the same evening once a contamination problem was found in it.**

The day started well. The first act's remaining scenes were finalised, and the series' overall shape got a significant rewrite around its central theme, with the ending decided concretely enough to plan backward from.

Then the whole second act, some sixty-five scenes, was drafted in one push, run through the project's canon-checking and editorial lenses as it went. Late that evening, using the overlap checker built the day before, David and Claude found that the new draft was too close to older, previously rejected material, close enough that it read as recycled rather than freshly written. The decision was blunt. The entire act was pulled and moved into a folder marked "do not open", and a rule blocking that specific kind of contamination went straight into the project's voice guide, in a section later drafting sessions would have to check themselves against.

Two of that act's scenes were rewritten from a clean brief the same night to confirm the new process actually worked, and it did. The sealed answer files were also reorganised so that editing them routes through a subagent, which keeps their contents out of the visible commit history this site's transcripts are built from.

David then prepared a detailed handoff document for the next session, setting out the brief for redrafting the second and third acts under all of that day's decisions, and reworked the top-level planning file into an orientation document rather than a working scratchpad.
