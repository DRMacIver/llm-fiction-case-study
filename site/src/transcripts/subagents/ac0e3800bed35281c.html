## The device

A second round would be about the parcel's disposal: the box is a plot-bearing object that sits on the page as a decision Edwin narrates to himself and never acts on, and the direction turned to the wall is the scene contradicting his stated plan without knowing it — a man who means to post something on does not hide its address from himself.

To make it work, the disposal has to be one act with a date attached and a reason for the turned face in the same beat: he recognises the box as sample work off a bench he wrote the instructions for, has no business opening another office's sample, decides it goes with the autumn letter, and turns the direction to the wall *because* it is a piece of somebody else's business he has agreed to be responsible for — which is the trap in one gesture. Otherwise cut his reasoning to the signature and let the box be inert on purpose.
