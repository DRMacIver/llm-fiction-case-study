## The two-line verdict

Mostly a conversation, and the spine of scene 02 is genuinely two people: Edwin's corrections take up Peri's actual words, and her theory eats each refutation, so every turn changes the state of the argument.

It tips into saying-things-at-each-other twice — the Weave monologue (L43) and her three-things summation (L81), both composed paragraphs no listener prompted, delivered at length to a man who has asked a narrow question — and both are exactly where Peri picks up Edwin's grammar (the balanced verdict half, the "and" pile) and loses her own.
