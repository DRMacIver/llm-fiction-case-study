Report on `draft/book-01/scenes/18-nought-in-every-kind.md`.

**1. "She came and listened. He watched her do it…"** — Part 1, pronoun with no clear antecedent (*"'You' — It's 'I'"*). As a scene file, 18 opens on an unnamed "she" and "he" and does not name Sorrel until paragraph six or Edwin until paragraph seven. Scene 17 supplies the antecedent, but the split falls mid-exchange, which means the opening of 18 reads as a fragment rather than an opening (Part 7, openings are engines). Replacement: `Sorrel came and listened. Edwin watched her do it…` — or move the split to before "Sorrel. Come and listen to this," so 18 opens on Edwin's line.

**2. "'Nothing left this block,' said Sorrel. 'There was nothing in it to leave.'"** — Part 1 (a word doing a job it cannot do) and Part 2 (the gesture at meaning). *Left* reads first as "remained" and only resolves to "departed" on the second clause; and the second clause is the first clause turned over, which is epigram shape rather than a new claim. It also fails Part 4: she is one beat past asking "Where are the worms?" and states as fact the conclusion the whole rest of the scene is built to earn. Replacement: `"Nothing died on that road," said Sorrel. "There was nothing in here to die."` — plainer, and it is an inference from the absent corpses she has just failed to find, which is what she actually has.

**3. redacted sentence: unpublished prose Nothing can be *driven out* of a corded, sealed box; and *killed* is what the following "Not dead worms" exists to rule out, so the clause pre-empts its own answer. Replacement: `and went through the straw and the bottom of the box with their fingers, methodically, looking for what a long dry road kills: nothing.` (The clause is inherited verbatim from ch. 005, which drew no comment — flagged because the check is per-sentence, not because it is new.)

**4. redacted sentence: unpublished prose Five revealings found nothing wrong; the sixth did not fail to see it — it returned nought, i.e. it saw the thing exactly. The sentence as written says the block beat all six, which the scene has just shown it did not. Replacement: `"Nothing gets to be invisible to five and honest to the sixth."` — or, keeping his voice, `"Nothing gets past the whole set."` Same inheritance caveat as (3).

**5. The scene's last line — "…and I have no spell that asks *why*, because I never imagined the question."** — Part 7, endings are cuts, not cadences. In ch. 005 this line sat mid-chapter with Sorrel's "You've been frightened before" after it; as scene 18's final sentence it becomes the most quotable line in the scene, italicised, in the last two paragraphs, which is precisely the shape the rule forbids. Fix at the level of redacted: place, not the words: run the scene one beat further and end on Sorrel's flat professional question or on her hand going back to the grey book, so the aphorism is passed over rather than landed on.

**6. No author's note in 17 or 18.** — Part 7, notes attach where the thing is *worked*.
