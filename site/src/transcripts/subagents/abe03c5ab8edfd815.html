Does the argument read as a conversation?

It reads as a conversation, and that is the scene's real strength: every turn takes up a word or a claim from the turn before it (Pending → It'll fill in; I did try asking → You announced → I announced and then I listened; least reassuring → You're welcome), and no one answers a question that wasn't asked.

What keeps it from being fully lived-in is that both parties receive every line perfectly and reply at its best reading, in unbroken alternation, with the two longest speeches (L31, L37) landing as prepared set-pieces rather than as things said badly under pressure — which is exactly where Peri's missing tally and Edwin's portable closer are doing the damage.
