## Hard walls

The constraints a naive route-1 LitRPG theory of magic will break first.
Each states exactly what the sealed material commits to.

1. **Mana is not a fluid, and nothing is powered by it.** deeps.md →
Mechanism 1: "Nothing is made of light and nothing is made of magic."
A Deep's entire productivity is chemical. No sealed file anywhere
posits a magical energy reservoir, leak, or concentration.
2. **Dungeons are not built and have no makers.** deeps.md → Mechanism
9: "People improved a natural hole; nobody made a Deep."
m2-deep-theme-critique.md: "no civilisation, no lost golden age, no
transmitted schedule."
3. **Nothing lives at the bottom.** deeps.md → Mechanism 1: "No maker.
No design. No intelligence. No boss." No boss fight, no revelation
scene, ever.
4. **The System did not make and does not know about dungeons.**
deeps.md → Mechanism 11 / COHERENCE.md → §2: it "does not make
dungeons, has not noticed them, and gives no telemetry about them."
Delving is ordinary hard practice.
5. **The System is not an artefact, agent, or god — it is retrospective
bookkeeping.** destiny.md → §2: "It never predicts and it never
assigns… it never says what it counted." m1-destiny-theme-review.md →
Objection 3: "the System an institution rather than a god."
6. **Nothing is chosen, marked, or reserved.** peri-pre-choosing.md →
§3: "**nothing selected her.**" Uniqueness reads as luck and "luck
does not have a mechanism"; it is never explained, anywhere, ever.
7. **No zero-sum luck and no third-party cost.** destiny.md → §4: "Luck
in this world is not conserved and nobody else's is spent."
Forbidden outright in destiny.md → Can no longer happen.
8. **The System never punishes.** destiny.md → Can no longer happen:
"Any System penalty, tightening, countdown, warning or threat."
`Terms:` is duration; refusal is free; fulfilment is free.
9. **Fate magic has no prescience and no planner.** destiny.md → §4:
"There is no prescience anywhere in this and no planner." The engine
nudges by a factor of two or three, locally, only toward a bound
destiny, and never touches evidence.
10. **The parish ceiling: no spell does anything with no unmagical
version.** silent-soil.md → Why magic reads it this way. Spells
induce or accelerate real processes; "The System gives no free
information."
11. **No spell reads presence, life, or a body's interior.**
silent-soil.md → Canon rules: the revealings ask a census question
or a rate question; healing is the church's oathbound mystery;
"nothing reads whether a body is spending in the ground."
12. **Blessing-sense is an impression and must never become a
detector.** silent-soil.md → Why magic reads it this way. It is
unquantified, untransferable, unrepeatable, and false-positive prone.
13. **No great evil, no single cause, no source to defeat.**
COHERENCE.md → §2: "There is no great evil anywhere in the sealed
material." m2-deep-theme-critique.md → Objection 2: "no single thing
to defeat, and no source."
14. **The soil never acts.** silent-soil.md → The real biology it stands
on: "It does not act, reach, choose, or grow. Its only verbs are
hold and spread." No working, no vector, no line on a map.
