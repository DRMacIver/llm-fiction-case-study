The scene has just established that the outer arc's leading edge is 9 chains 50 (209 yards) *from* the thorn stump on the north headland; the arcs therefore do not reach the headland, they are two hundred yards short of it, and the sentence contradicts the measurement the whole scene was built to take. This is also where redacted: place "two hundred days" passage stood: with it gone, the jump to Sennet's grass has no bridge and the remaining clause has swollen to fill the hole. Replacement: *"But that stump on the north headland is two hundred yards from the edge of the outer arc, and at a yard a day that is about two hundred days. The far side of that redacted: character is Mr Sennet's grass, and nobody can say what a thing like this does under old grass without digging some. His ground, his leave. I'll ask him tomorrow."*

**"Then he moved both hands six inches closer together and stayed again, and again, until his thumbs nearly touched." / "I can bracket it to about a foot ...
