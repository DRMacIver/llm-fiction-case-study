## The two devices a second round would be about

1. **Discovery arriving as scenery rather than as somebody's act** — the register open on the counter in 99, Peri's notebook read past her shoulder in 100. Both hand the reader a fact nobody in the room chose to give. 2. **The pen as punctuation** — three of the last four scenes end on someone writing, and in 100 the writing stands in for a reaction. It works when writing is the work (97's redacted: character writing the third no against her own wish is the good version); it fails when it is a caption on a good line.
