Device that a second round of comments would be about: the descriptions are being inserted as *lists of attributes attached to a name* — appearance-file bullets converted to prose and parked wherever the character first appears, rather than things the viewpoint notices because he has a reason to notice them. Every finding above is a symptom of that one construction. To make it work, each insertion should be bound to what is already happening in its sentence (Sorrel's stillness shown by her not moving; Sepp's wrist shown when she uses the trowel; Peri's size shown by the kit not fitting), and no character should be described twice.

Second device: Edwin's own appearance. Scene 01 currently solves the viewpoint problem by narrating his face through a frame that cannot carry it. The only legitimate versions are what he handles or hears about himself (the badly cut hair he knows is badly cut, the robe, the hands) and what other people *do* in response to looking at him. Anything about his face has to come from a character's mouth or go.
